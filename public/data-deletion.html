What gets deleted

When you remove Shattered Saga from your Facebook or Google account, or ask us directly, we delete your account record and everything linked to it: your email address, sign-in identities, active sessions, subscription tier and turn balance.

Character saves and adventure progress are stored locally in your own browser, not on our servers. Clearing your browser data removes those.

Requesting deletion yourself

You can request deletion at any time by emailing acalistudios@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We action requests within 30 days.

Via Facebook

Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, select Shattered Saga, and choose Remove. Facebook notifies us automatically and your data is deleted.

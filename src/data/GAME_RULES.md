## Prologue: The Shattered Realm

### The Fivefold Font & The Ancient Calamity
Long before the rise of current frontier settlements, the central realm of Aethelgard was governed under an ancient covenant of elemental balance. At its spiritual heart stood **The Fivefold Font**—a sacred circular stone shrine where the five foundational elements of creation (**Air, Earth, Fire, Water, and Aether**) were held in sacred equilibrium. Elemental affinity was revered as a solemn civic responsibility rather than a tool for conquest.

Human ambition unraveled that golden balance. In a disastrous bid for absolute dominion, five champions entered the shrine and attempted to seize every element simultaneously, seeking to bind all five primordial currents into mortal flesh. The font cracked down its central axis, fracturing the spiritual unity of the land and leaving the guardians of each element alienated, each convinced its own virtue was the only one worthy of survival.

The cataclysm tore imperial borders apart into **The Shattered Realm**: a rugged, dangerous continent where law is maintained by steel and grit, trade roads are preyed upon by deserters and goblins, and ancient elemental ruins stir once more with rising planar static.

### The Five Cosmic Elements
- **Air (Swift & Free):** Masters of speed, perception, wind currents, and ranged marksmanship.
- **Earth (Resilient & Dense):** Anchored in endurance, heavy stone, physical fortitude, and craftsmanship.
- **Fire (Volatile & Fierce):** Driven by passion, explosive martial strength, courage, and searing combustion.
- **Water (Flowing & Adaptable):** Bound to healing, empathy, fluid movement, and spiritual cleansing.
- **Aether (Ethereal & Planar):** Attuned to the metaphysical weave, sacred relics, spatial seams, and memory.

## Chapter 1: Quick Start Guide & The Core Loop

### The Interactive Narrative Cycle
1. **Narrative Presentation:** The Chronicler describes the immediate tactical scene, NPC dialogue, and environmental hazards.
2. **Player Intent:** Declare your character's reaction using suggested choices or freeform natural language typing. Use quotation marks for spoken dialogue: `"Stand back from the forge, Vance!"`
3. **Step-Die Opposed Check:** When attempting challenging or uncertain feats, the client throws the opposed dice:
   `Player Roll = Primary Attribute Die + Secondary Attribute Die + (Skill Ranks × 1d2) + Modifiers vs. Challenge Resistance Roll`
4. **Resolution & State Update:** The Chronicler narrates the dramatic outcome based on your Margin of Success, while the engine updates Hit Points, Fatigue, Inventory, Quests, and Currency.

### The Turn & Time Economy
- Each tactical turn represents approximately **10 seconds** of combat or **10–15 minutes** of dungeon/wilderness exploration.
- You may perform **one Primary Action** (attack, cast a spell, pick a lock, sprint) alongside reasonable **Free Actions** (drawing a blade, battle cry, dropping an item).
- At **18:00 dusk**, the engine prompts for your daily evening meal, consuming 1 ration from your pack per 24 hours.

## Chapter 2: Creating Your Champion

### The 14 Canonical Professions
- **Bandit:** Cunning highwayman; skilled in Light Weapons, Stealth, Thievery, Escapology, and Intimidation.
- **Bard:** Traveling skald and poet; skilled in Performance, Deception, Lore, Insight, and Negotiation.
- **Cleric:** Ordained chapel minister; skilled in Divine Communion, Divine Manifestation, Healing, Lore, and Leadership.
- **Craftsman:** Artisan builder; skilled in Crafting, Smithing, Appraise, Athletics, and Perception.
- **Duelist:** Disciplined blade fencer; skilled in Light Weapons, Acrobatics, Insight, Brawling, and Deception.
- **Farmer:** Hardy tiller; skilled in Survival, Herbalism, Animal Rapport, Athletics, and Brawling.
- **Healer:** Field apothecary; skilled in Healing, Herbalism, Alchemy, Insight, and Divine Communion.
- **Hunter:** Wilderness tracker; skilled in Marksmanship, Tracking, Stealth, Survival, and Trapping.
- **Merchant:** Shrewd trader; skilled in Negotiation, Appraise, Deception, Insight, and Languages.
- **Sailor:** Veteran coastal mariner; skilled in Athletics, Acrobatics, Brawling, Survival, and Escapology.
- **Scholar:** Academic researcher; skilled in Lore, Languages, Insight, Arcane Drawing, and Appraise.
- **Shaman:** Elemental seer; skilled in Divine Communion, Arcane Shaping, Herbalism, Animal Rapport, and Survival.
- **Soldier:** Castle garrison infantry; skilled in Heavy Weapons, Blocking, Athletics, Leadership, and Intimidation.
- **Wilderness Guide:** Trail scout; skilled in Survival, Tracking, Perception, Marksmanship, and Athletics.

### The Eight Core Attributes
- **Power:** Raw muscular strength, melee impact, and athletic force.
- **Coordination:** Agility, manual dexterity, balance, and precision.
- **Vigor:** Physical constitution, wound resistance, and stamina.
- **Willpower:** Mental discipline, courage, resolve, and resistance to terror.
- **Intellect:** Analytical reasoning, tactical memory, and logic.
- **Charisma:** Presence, persuasion, leadership, and force of personality.
- **Attunement:** Sensitivity to elemental energy, leylines, and the weave.
- **Empathy:** Intuitive connection with others, psychological insight, and divine piety.

### Multi-Dimensional Roleplay Modifiers (-3 to +3)
The Chronicler evaluates your actions and dialogue across three moral dimensions:
1. **Virtue Alignment:** +1 for upholding declared virtue under pressure; -1 for direct violation.
2. **Vice Temptation:** +1 for struggling against or mastering vice in character; -1 for mindless surrender.
3. **Philosophical Conviction:** +1 for acting in accordance with declared philosophy; -1 for acting against core creed.
- **Net Modifier:** Stacks from **-3 to +3**, flashing on-screen and logging in the Action Log to modify your next roll.

### Morality Tiers & Combat Advantages
- **Morality >= +20 (Virtuous):** +1 Attack Roll and +1 Raw Damage vs Demonic, Undead, and Monstrous foes.
- **Morality >= +40 (Saintly):** +2 Attack Roll and +2 Raw Damage vs Demonic, Undead, and Monstrous foes.
- **Morality >= +60 (Exalted):** +3 Attack Roll and +3 Raw Damage vs Demonic, Undead, and Monstrous foes.
- **Morality <= -20 (Cruel):** +1 Attack Roll and +1 Raw Damage vs Innocent, Guard, and Merchant targets.
- **Morality <= -40 (Vile):** +2 Attack Roll and +2 Raw Damage vs Innocent, Guard, and Merchant targets.
- **Morality <= -60 (Scourge):** +3 Attack Roll and +3 Raw Damage vs Innocent, Guard, and Merchant targets.

## Chapter 4: The Opposed Roll Engine

### The Step-Die Progression
- **Score 1 (Untrained):** Primary `1d4`, Secondary `1d2`
- **Score 2 (Average):** Primary `1d6`, Secondary `1d2 + 1`
- **Score 3 (Practiced):** Primary `1d8`, Secondary `1d4`
- **Score 4 (Veteran):** Primary `1d10`, Secondary `1d4 + 1`
- **Score 5 (Master):** Primary `1d12`, Secondary `1d6`
- **Score 6+ (Legendary):** Primary `1d20`, Secondary `1d10`

### Margins of Success
`Margin = Player Roll - Challenge Resistance Roll`
- **Critical Triumph (+8 or higher):** Spectacular success with bonus tactical perks.
- **Standard Success (+1 to +7):** Decisive execution of intent.
- **Dynamic Standoff (0 — Tie):** Tense stalemate, locked weapons, or temporary delay.
- **Failure with Complication (-1 to -7):** Clean failure, or success achieved at severe cost.
- **Catastrophic Fumble (-8 or lower):** Disastrous mishap, broken weapon, or ambush.

## Chapter 5: Combat & Tactical Warfare

### Active Defenses & Soak
- **Acrobatics (Dodge):** Completely avoids all incoming damage on success.
- **Blocking (Parry/Shield):** Reduces damage based on shield size (Wooden: 1d4, Medium Iron: 1d6+1, Heavy Tower: 1d8+2).
- **Ward (Willpower/Arcane):** Resists mental fear, demonic curses, and raw planar energy.
- **Armor Soak:** Light (1d3), Medium (1d4+1), Heavy (1d6+2).

### The Multi-Defense Penalty (-2 Cumulative)
Defending against multiple strikes in the same round imposes a cumulative **-2 penalty**:
- 1st Defense: Normal roll
- 2nd Defense: -2 penalty
- 3rd Defense: -4 penalty
- 4th Defense: -6 penalty

## Chapter 6: Freeform Magic: Arcane & Divine

- **Arcane Energy (`arcaneSP`):** Powers *Arcane Drawing* and *Arcane Shaping*.
- **Divine Energy (`divineSP`):** Powers *Divine Communion* and *Divine Manifestation*.
- **Burnout:** Channeling magic without energy consumes character Fatigue and Hit Points directly.

## Chapter 7: Survival, Time & Travel

- **Daily Meals:** 1 ration consumed at **18:00 dusk** every 24 hours.
- **Full Camp (with ration):** Restores 50 Fatigue, fully restores Arcane & Divine Energy, heals 25% Max HP.
- **Starving Camp (no rations):** Restores 15 Fatigue, restores half magic energy, heals 0 HP.
- **Safe Tavern Inn:** Completely clears all Fatigue, resets magic energy to maximum, heals 50% Max HP.
- **Scaled Encumbrance:**
  - *Tier 1 (1–3 slots over):* -1 Acrobatics, +2 Fatigue/turn, no sprinting.
  - *Tier 2 (4–6 slots over):* -3 all physical checks, +5 Fatigue/turn, speed halved.
  - *Tier 3 (7+ slots over):* Immobile / Encumbered Stasis.
- **Darkness & Torches:** Pitch darkness imposes -4 penalty. Torches illuminate 15 yards (normal melee), but distant targets beyond light circle suffer -2 dim-light penalty.

---

## Chapter 8: Health, Injury & Resurrection

- **Hit Points (HP) & Fatigue:** 0 HP triggers Dying status. 70+ Fatigue imposes -2 penalty; 100 Fatigue causes collapse.
- **Venous Bleeding:** Wiping with a cloth does nothing. Stopped only by **sustained direct pressure** (occupies both hands for 2 rounds), **First Aid bandages**, or **Water/Divine healing**. Stabilizing at 1 HP while actively bleeding will cause re-collapse on the next bleed tick.
- **Gear Trails:** Dropped equipment remains at the site of death as a marked Gear Trail awaiting recovery.

## Chapter 10: The AI Game Master & Interactive Play

- **The Voice of The Chronicler:** Neutral, grounded narrator addressing player directly as `"you"`. Focuses on sensory reality and tactical clarity.
- **Quoted Dialogue:** Always enclose character speech in quotation marks: `"Drop the keys, Vance."`
- **Speaking Directly to the GM (`[OOC:]`):** Use `[OOC: ...]` or `((out of character))` to correct misunderstandings or clarify intent without breaking character.
- **Anti-Godmoding:** Rule 10 prohibits declaring direct outcomes. Always declare intent and roll the opposed dice.
- **Action Log & Visual Alerts:** Roleplay bonuses/penalties flash on-screen and record in the dedicated in-game Action Log.

## Chapter 11: Downtime, Economy & Training

- **Currency:** 100 cp = 1 sp; 10 sp = 1 gp (1 gp = 1,000 cp).
- **50% Resale Floor:** Merchants buy loot at 50% listed value; Negotiation checks improve prices up to 25%.
